## Known conflict

In D3_HA501_6.SAR2026.pdf, pages 22-23 include emergency-department triage error among the nine essential patient-safety areas, while the infographic on page 24 substitutes pressure injury/falls. The official SAR 2026 form explicitly includes under-triage and over-triage in emergency care. Therefore:

- use emergency triage for the current SAR 2026 essential set;
- treat the page-24 infographic as inconsistent or from another version;
- re-verify if HAI issues a later official form or announcement.
