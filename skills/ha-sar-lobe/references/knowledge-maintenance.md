# Knowledge Maintenance Protocol

This lobe is a curated second brain, not an indiscriminate document store.

## Knowledge classes

Classify every durable addition as one of:

- **Normative:** official standard, form, announcement, scoring rule, or required indicator.
- **Interpretive:** official training explanation or surveyor guidance.
- **Writing pattern:** reusable way to express context, 3P, learning, plan, or results.
- **Scoring lesson:** evidence that distinguishes maturity levels.
- **Hospital-specific:** facts, systems, results, and decisions belonging to one hospital.
- **Conflict/uncertainty:** inconsistent sources, version questions, or unresolved interpretation.

Never merge hospital-specific content into normative knowledge.

## Required provenance

Record:

- exact source title and filename/URL;
- issuer/author when known;
- publication or effective date;
- version and pages/sections;
- date added;
- authority class;
- concise durable claim;
- whether superseded, disputed, or pending verification.

## Update rules

- Newer is not automatically more authoritative; official current rules outrank later informal slides.
- Keep a conflict note until the discrepancy is resolved.
- When a normative source changes, identify which writing/scoring rules are affected.
- Preserve prior versions only when needed to interpret an older SAR or survey period.
- Do not memorize transient hospital numbers as general knowledge. Keep them in the hospital-specific evidence package with period and source.

## Retrieval rules

Retrieve only the references needed for the current chapter and mode. Begin with the source register for version questions. For hospital drafting, retrieve hospital evidence before generic writing patterns.

## Learning from completed work

After a SAR section is reviewed or tested in a survey, add only durable lessons:

- what evidence proved decisive;
- what wording caused ambiguity;
- what scoring assumption was corrected;
- what result or tracer exposed a system gap;
- what pattern can safely generalize beyond that hospital.

Avoid preserving stylistic preferences as hard rules unless the user explicitly adopts them.
