# SAR Writing Patterns

## Evidence-first preparation

Before drafting, create a compact evidence map.

| Claim or requirement | Evidence source and location | Evidence type | Date/period | What it supports | Gap/conflict | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example claim | File/page/table/interview | Design / Action / Learning / Improve / Result | YYYY-MM | Exact claim supported | Missing coverage or conflicting source | Confirmed / Provisional / Missing |

Do not cite a policy as proof of implementation or an activity as proof of effectiveness.

## SPA-to-SAR conversion

For Parts I-III, use the matching SPA 2022 section as a structured enquiry aid after identifying the applicable current overall requirement:

1. Decode the HA Standard 6 purpose and important issues.
2. Map the chapter to SPA 2022 by topic, not code alone.
3. Turn SPA `กิจกรรมที่ควรดำเนินการ` into questions about design, action, coverage, control, and learning.
4. Turn SPA `ประเด็นที่ควรพิจารณา/ตอบในการประเมินตนเอง` into an evidence request split into practice and result.
5. Retrieve hospital evidence and mark what is confirmed, missing, conflicting, or only proposed.
6. Synthesize the supported material into Context, 3P, learning/improvement, results, and the 1-2 year plan required by SAR 2026.

SPA activities are suggestions for contextual selection, not extra mandatory requirements. Do not paste them as an activity inventory or present their presence as proof of maturity. Read [spa-2022-guidance.md](spa-2022-guidance.md) for edition controls and chapter pages.

## Minimum source inventory

For a full hospital SAR, look for the relevant subset of:

- current Hospital Profile and strategy;
- prior SAR and prior survey findings;
- policies, flowcharts, work instructions, and committee governance;
- incident/RCA/Risk Register evidence;
- audits, tracers, chart review, observation, and interview evidence;
- KPI definitions, raw numerator/denominator where needed, trends, and benchmarks;
- CLT/PCT profiles and important disease/procedure results;
- workforce, patient experience, financial, and system-specific evidence;
- ENV findings for II-3;
- completed improvement cycles and spread/integration evidence.

## Context pattern

Write context as a decision-relevant causal frame:

> จาก [สถานการณ์/ข้อมูล/ความเสี่ยง] ซึ่งส่งผลต่อ [ผู้ป่วย/บุคลากร/ความต่อเนื่อง/เป้าหมายองค์กร] โรงพยาบาลจึงกำหนด [ประเด็นสำคัญหรือโจทย์การออกแบบ] โดยมีข้อจำกัด/ความท้าทายสำคัญคือ [ข้อเท็จจริง].

Good context is selective. It changes how the reader interprets the process and results.

## 3P development pattern

> **Purpose:** เพื่อให้เกิด [value/outcome] ใน [target population/system] โดยมุ่งลด/ยกระดับ [material risk or performance gap].
>
> **Process:** โรงพยาบาลออกแบบ [system] โดย [critical design choices]. นำไปปฏิบัติใน [coverage/setting], กำหนด [roles/controls/escalation], และติดตามด้วย [measures/review mechanism].
>
> **Performance and learning:** ผลการดำเนินงานพบว่า [outcome/trend/variation]. การทบทวนพบ [lesson or residual gap] จึงปรับ [specific system change], ส่งผลให้ [later result or coverage].

Do not print the labels Purpose/Process/Performance in final prose when a coherent paragraph is clearer.

## Plan pattern

> จาก [evidence of gap/risk/variation] ในระยะ [timeframe] โรงพยาบาลจะ [specific system change] ครอบคลุม [scope], โดยมีเป้าหมาย [intended result] และติดตามด้วย [measure/milestone].

Avoid vague plans: “ดำเนินการต่อเนื่อง,” “พัฒนาศักยภาพ,” or “กำกับติดตาม” without a specified change and measure.

## Part IV result pattern

For each important result:

1. define the measure and denominator if needed;
2. show target, current value, and trend;
3. compare with benchmark or internal best when meaningful;
4. annotate major interventions and context changes;
5. distinguish common-cause from special-cause variation when data allow;
6. state what the organization learned and changed;
7. link the result to the relevant process chapter.

## Rewrite discipline

When revising existing SAR:

- preserve unique context, good evidence, and meaningful results;
- remove duplicated standard text and low-value activity inventories;
- do not upgrade certainty or score language beyond the evidence;
- use exact denominators, coverage, periods, and comparison groups;
- keep “what was done” separate from “what happened because of it”;
- mark missing values instead of inserting plausible numbers.
